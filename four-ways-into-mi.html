Station 4 · Complications & teaching

What goes wrong, and when

Scan left to right. Damaged muscle is electrically unstable early, then mechanically weak, then inflamed and scarred.

First 24 hours

  • Dysrhythmias: PVCs, VT, VF. VF is the top cause of early death.
  • Heart blocks, especially with inferior MI.
  • Cardiogenic shock when ~40% of LV is lost.

Days 1–7

  • Heart failure: crackles, S3, JVD.
  • Pericarditis: sharp pain worse lying flat, friction rub.
  • Papillary muscle rupture: new murmur, sudden pulmonary edema.
  • Wall rupture: sudden collapse, tamponade.

Weeks later

  • Dressler syndrome: pericarditis, fever, 2–10 weeks out.
  • Ventricular aneurysm in the scarred wall.
  • Mural thrombus: stroke risk.

Home nitro, at a glance

STEP 1Stop. Sit or lie down.
STEP 21 tablet under the tongue.
5 MINNot better? Call 911.
STEP 3May repeat every 5 min. Max 3 tablets.

Keep tablets in the original dark glass bottle. Headache is expected. No sildenafil, tadalafil or vardenafil with nitrates.
